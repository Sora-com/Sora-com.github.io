I often dwell on about the characteristics that make a person and, if one's development was completely conscious, how would I choose to develop myself. 
I am aware that one's environment says quite nearly all you must know about someone. That's why there are plenty of innocent people in prison because of their circumstances. If you learned that someone was murdered, and the two suspects were an ex-alcoholic man, with a past envolving drugs and violence history; and a clean, responsible man coming from a well-structured home, you would probably think it was the first. 

Can you escape your environment? Yes. 
It is undoubtedly hard. But I believe that the key to live happily, and free from the chains of where you were born, is to be **self-conscious** and **self-loved**, and *that* is what I mean by self-reliance, and that's how you will never be alone. 

# Be aware and expose yourself

I believe the first step is being observant and to praise critical thinking above else. Analyze your environment and get a hold of what environments other people you know get involved into. How is your home compared to one of an alcoholic father's? How is your home compared to the one from that friend? Read some books. Analyze the unspoken rules among the characters, even if it is a fantasy world. Maybe they praise family and tradition. Do you like that principle? Do you feel aligned with it? Is it something present in your actual environment? 

It is important to note that you mustn't stay wwith the values you see are popular because of so-called 'trends'. Nobody knows what makes the perfect man. Do whatever makes you feel worth living and act in a way you feel alive. Be it whatever it be. 

> 'Do not waste time dwelling on what the perfect man is. Become one.' - Marcus Aurelius 

You will never see what is wrong with your environment if you never look outside the window. Be enthusiastic to learn about the many different ways people form families and households. 
This will teach you to recognise the distinct behaviours of your household and make you conscious when you eventually show them as well, and thus you will be able to **freely** choose if you wish to continue on those values or not. That is where freedom comes from. Freedom comes from becoming what you've always wished to become. 

We are human, and as humans we learn from our company. It is unavoidable. However, with this in mind, we can teach ourselves to be **self-conscious**, and critical, and thus, have the skills to choose for ourselves. 

# Choose your environment 

The absolute key to never feeling alone in the world is to find your kin. Find the people that make you feel as if you were living a '*slice of life*'. When you find that, you will live every day knowing that you will remember those days with sweet nostalgia. 

These are the people you will grow up and learn from, so they will determine your character. Find the people you admire, and tell them. Be expressive with all the positive thoughts you hold, and happiness will come your way. 

In the same way, you must do your best to stray away from those you recognise (because of the comparison you learned before) are not good for you. You must be brave enough. You must **love** yourself enough to know that there is always something better waiting for you. The best things in life always hide behind fear. 

Let the people that don't hold your values go away. Don't punish them, don't hate them. Just understand that they are *not* meant to be with you. 

The environment you choose shouldn't be an echo chamber, it should be diverse. But some values are necessary that you share with the other people in order to work towards the same direction. There is no use in rowing a boat when some row forwards and others row backwards. Avoid relationships with people who lack the values needed for communication and growth, and don't expect them to evolve. You are not responsible for them. Everyone is responsible for themselves, except for the people you brought into the world. 

#  You are your best friend 

What if all fails? How does one survive each situation and hardship in life? When you are all alone, with no noise around you. Whenever you feel lost, who is that person that will always talk to you? Yourself. 

Become your best friend. 
You are the only person in the world that knows youself the best. 
You've been there since the start. 
You know all the things you've been through.
You know how far you've come. 
You are the **only** one that will **always** have your back. 

Hug yourself. Thank yourself for always being there. Love yourself for the jokes you think, for the good things you've worked for yourself. For the friends you've made thanks to yourself. 

Gratitude towards youself will take you far, because you *know* you owe yourself. 
If you think you have nothing to be grateful for, and if you feel like you are on bad terms with yourself, then make an effort to forgive yourself. Deeply and truly forgive. You are the only self you have. Take a deep breath. You will *always* work it out, and if not, it *will* pass. 

In order to ease this process, get used to thinking and talking to yourself. 

When you have yourself, you can find your family. If that's the family you were born into, great. You will just have a stronger bond to them. If not, you will find it. Everyone needs one. You just have to take an active part in your life. 
Work towards it. 

Happiness doesn't come unconsciously. You are ready. Go get it. I love you.
